# Petal

A pastel browser snake game with rival snakes, cheering bubbles, confetti, and optional sound. No build step or dependencies required.

Open `index.html` in your browser, or run `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this directory and visit http://localhost:8000.

Move your mouse over the garden or drag your finger to steer in any direction. The snake curves smoothly toward your target. Arrow keys and WASD also steer; hold two directions for diagonals. Space pauses, and mobile direction buttons are available too.

Collect berries for 10 points and catch smaller snakes for 50 points. Edges wrap; touching your own body ends the run. Your personal best is saved in your browser when local storage is available.

NPC snakes spawn with random lengths of 2–8 segments and wander with curved, free-angle movement. Only snakes shorter than yours can be eaten.
